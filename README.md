 
# 🧠 Tumor Growth Research (Ongoing)

## Overview
This repository documents my involvement in an **ongoing collaborative research project** within the **ADAPTED Research Group**, focused on mathematical modeling, prediction, and advanced control of tumor growth dynamics.

The research includes the development of a framework titled **"A Physics-Informed Neural Networks Framework for Mechanistic and Data-Driven Tumor Growth Prediction"**, combining mechanistic mathematical models with Physics-Informed Neural Networks (PINNs).

The project is under active development, with ongoing research extending toward advanced control strategies for tumor growth models as part of my bachelor's thesis.

## Research Scope
- Mathematical modeling and simulation of tumor growth dynamics
- Physics-Informed Neural Networks (PINNs)
- Mechanistic and data-driven tumor growth prediction
- Time-series analysis and scientific computing
- Investigation of advanced control strategies for dynamic tumor models
